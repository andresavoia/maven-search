What changes

Fixed chunks optimize for a uniform vector length. They cut whenever the word budget runs out, so the retrieved context is often a slice of two topics or the middle of one idea. Moment RAG first finds where the talk changes subject, keeps that span together, and retrieves the whole moment. Citations are a start and end time of a coherent stretch, not an arbitrary window.
