02 — Reference architecture

What Moment RAG is

The course Moment RAG does not retrieve a document. It retrieves a moment: a contiguous stretch of a talk, with a start and an end. The citation jumps the player to that timestamp. The reference pipeline is self-query, decompose, hybrid retrieve (dense + BM25 + HyDE), cross-encoder rerank, then a cited answer.

Unit

A moment, not a token window.

Locator

A real timestamp from retrieval. The model does not invent one.

This build

Same idea, smaller stack: topic cuts, dense + BM25, quoted answer.

04 — Part 2

Moment RAG

36

moments

~28s

beats, then topic cuts

50–180s

moment length after merge and split

  • Pack cues into beats. Embed each beat. Cut where neighboring beats stop matching.
  • Score a moment by its best inner beat plus a BM25 score on the full moment text.
  • Keep one previous beat of overlap so the thesis sentence is not left on the wrong side of the cut.
  • The answer quotes that moment and cites its start and end.

07 — Findings

Limits and next steps

Findings

  • Fixed chunks miss a stage that starts a few minutes later. RLHF is the clear case.
  • When the answer is one sentence in the opening, the chunk is tighter and the moment drags in the setup.
  • A citation is checkable: the player jumps to the timestamp.

Limits

  • Auto captions. Moment titles are spoken fragments.
  • A long moment dilutes one embedding.
  • No cross-encoder. No HyDE.
  • Without an API key the answer is a quote, not a paraphrase. That is enough, and it cannot invent a time.

Next: a cross-encoder reranker, then an optional paraphrase that still may cite only retrieved times. Fly.io is not part of this submission.
